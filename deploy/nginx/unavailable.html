This dashboard isn’t answering right now

The web server is running, but the dashboard behind it isn’t responding. That usually means one of these:

  • It is being rebuilt or has just restarted, and is still starting up.
  • It was stopped on purpose.
  • It ran into an error and shut down.

Waiting a minute and reloading is worth trying first — a large dashboard can take a while to come back after a restart.

If you look after this instance: ./deploy/manage.sh health will say which of the three it is.
